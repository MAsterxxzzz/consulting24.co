Zofeur vs Chauffeur-With-Car — Questions

Zofeur facts in these answers come from zofeur.com, black.zofeur.com and their official app-store listings, captured 2026-08-29 — sources linked in the sections above.

Will you send a driver to drive my own car?

No — our driver comes with our car, a black Mercedes-Maybach S-Class. Driving your own car is the driver-on-call model that apps like Zofeur advertise; for that job, their product is the right shape.

What is the difference between Zofeur and a chauffeur service with a car?

As Zofeur's own site describes it, you hire their chauffeur to drive your car, pay-per-minute or in blocks. A chauffeur service with the car included — ours — supplies both the vehicle and the driver at fixed published rates: AED 1,600 one-way up to AED 10,000 for 10 hours.

Is this cheaper than Zofeur?

Almost certainly not, and we will not pretend otherwise: Zofeur shows in-app fare estimates for driving your own car by the minute, while our rates buy a chauffeured Mercedes-Maybach S-Class. Different products, different prices.

When is Zofeur the better choice?

When the car is already yours: a safe ride home in your own vehicle, garage or inspection runs, errands by the minute — the use cases their own homepage lists. When the car should come too, that is us.
